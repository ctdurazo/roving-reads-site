# RovingReads beta organizer quick-start

The founding beta is a chance for your club to try its shared bookshelf, ratings, and book recommendations. Please try the steps below with at least one other member so you can check that the invitation and shared shelf work for both of you.

1. **Install and sign in.** Open the beta link you received. Follow any setup instructions for your device, then create an account or sign in. If the link fails or your device is unsupported, reply to your beta invitation for help.
2. **Create your club.** Open **Clubs**, create a club with a name your members will recognize, then open it. Confirm its visibility with the beta team before adding anything you want to keep private.
3. **Invite a member.** Open **Invites**, create a member invitation, and copy the link. Send it directly to one member. Ask them to open it, sign in with the invited address if you specified one, and select **Accept invitation** to reach the club bookshelf.
4. **Add your first book.** Use **Search** to find a book by title or author. Open the matching book and add it to your club's bookshelf. Set its reading status and ask your invited member to confirm the book appears on their shelf.
5. **Try ratings and recommendations.** Open a shelf book to add your rating. Open **Recs**, request suggestions, and save one to the club shelf. If you reach a recommendation limit, you can keep using the bookshelf and try generating again when the app allows it.

## What to expect

Beta features may change as clubs try them. Keep using your usual tools for annual reading selection, calendars, and meetings during the beta. Read the privacy information supplied with your invitation before inviting members, especially if your club shares ratings.

## Questions and feedback

For bugs, ideas, or questions, open **Settings → Beta feedback** and choose a category. If you cannot open the app or send the draft, use the support address in your beta invitation. Use that address for privacy concerns. For a bug, include your device platform, app version if available, what you were trying to do, and what happened. If you or a member cannot join or use the club, mention that in your message so the beta team can prioritize it. Keep passwords, invitation links, and other members' information out of feedback.
