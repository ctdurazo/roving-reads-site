Beta feedback

Report a bug, share an idea or ask a question. Please leave out passwords, invitation links, private club discussions, book recommendations and rating history.

For bugs, include the steps, what you expected, what happened, and whether you can continue using the app.

Reports are stored for up to 90 days and reviewed by the RovingReads beta team to improve the app. Email is collected only if you request a reply. App details are optional; no account or club identity is attached automatically. Avoid entering sensitive information.

Cannot sign in or continue? Contact beta support.
